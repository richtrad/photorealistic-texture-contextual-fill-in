# Manual-colourisation outputs

This directory contains nine presentation copies of manual-colourisation
images from the private Drive folders `kopali kolorovano` and its
`rr kolorovano` subfolder. They are separate from the PSD layer exports in
[`../layers/working_psd_exports/`](../layers/working_psd_exports/): these files
are finished manual-colourisation outputs, not individual Photoshop layers.

The public copies are PNGs with a maximum dimension of 4096 pixels. They were
created by bounded downscaling and lossless PNG encoding only; no colour
correction, retouching, sharpening, generative enhancement, or content edit
was applied. RGB/RGBA source modes are preserved. The complete transformation
and output hashes are in [`manifest.json`](manifest.json), while the Drive
folder and source-file provenance is in
[`../../metadata/manual-colorization-sources.json`](../../metadata/manual-colorization-sources.json).

The original Drive files, including the large source PNG/JPEG files and the
corresponding PSD/PSB working files, remain outside Git. This directory is a
derived presentation archive and should not be read as a complete release of
the raw historical-photo data. Historical photographs and other third-party
material may require separate attribution or permission.
